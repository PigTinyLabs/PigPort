How to Play iOS Games on Mac

On an Apple silicon Mac, start with the Mac App Store. If the game is missing and you have a compatible unencrypted .ipa, you can try PigPort. If the game is already installed on your iPhone, Apple’s iPhone Mirroring can let you play it from your Mac.

Install a game from the Mac App Store

Check Apple menu → About This Mac. A chip name such as Apple M1 or M4 means you have Apple silicon.

  1. Open the Mac App Store and search for the game. Choose iPhone & iPad Apps when the search filter is available.
  2. Look for Designed for iPhone or Designed for iPad, or choose the Mac edition if the publisher offers one.
  3. Click Get or the download button and open the game from Applications. For touch gestures, check the game’s settings in the menu bar for Touch Alternatives.

Install a compatible iOS game with PigPort

You’ll need an Apple silicon Mac and an unencrypted arm64 .ipa you have permission to install, such as a build provided by the developer. Game compatibility depends on the title and version.

  1. Download the macOS (Apple Silicon) build of PigPort from the official downloads.
  2. Open the disk image, drag PigPort to Applications, and open the app.
  3. In PigPort’s Game Library, drop the .ipa into the window or choose Install IPA / APK / XAPK. After installation, choose Play on the game card.
  4. Choose Controls on the game card to map keys. In supported games, press ⌘K to open the editor while playing.

Play a game installed on your iPhone

Open iPhone Mirroring on your Mac, follow the pairing prompts, and open the game in the mirrored iPhone window. The game runs on your nearby iPhone. Apple requires iOS 18 or later and macOS Sequoia 15 or later on an Apple silicon Mac or an Intel Mac with a T2 chip.

If the game won’t install or launch

The game is missing from the Mac App Store
Check whether the publisher offers a native Mac version. For a game installed on your iPhone, try iPhone Mirroring if your devices meet Apple’s requirements.
PigPort reports that the IPA is encrypted
PigPort needs a compatible unencrypted build. Ask the developer for a build they permit you to install, or use the App Store or iPhone Mirroring.
The game installs and then closes
Some games depend on iPhone-specific services or check the device environment. Check whether your game and version are supported before reinstalling or changing controls.
About This Mac says Intel
The Intel build of PigPort supports Android packages. To play an iOS game, check for a Mac edition or see if your Intel Mac supports iPhone Mirroring.
