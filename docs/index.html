Where to Find .IPA & .APK Game Files?

PigPort runs non-encrypted iOS packages and Android APK/XAPKs. Here are the top community repositories and search tips.

🍎

iOS Games (.IPA)

For Apple Silicon M1/M2/M3/M4
Decrypted IPAs
  • ✦ decrypt.day: The most popular database for decrypted iOS IPAs designed for macOS Apple Silicon. Simply search the game name and download the .ipa.
  • ✦ Google Search query: Search "[Game Name] decrypted ipa" or "[Game Name] ipa for mac".
  • ✦ Own Apple ID extraction: Export IPAs from your purchased apps with tools like Apple Configurator or IPA extraction utilities.

Step 1

Open Controls Editor

Press ⌘K (Command+K) anytime during gameplay, or click Controls on the title bar. PigPort captures a real-time snapshot of the game screen so you can place controls with pixel perfection.

Step 2

Add & Map Control Types

• WASD / Numpad Joystick: Drag onto the game movement pad.
• Key Buttons: Double-click to assign keyboard keys or mouse clicks (LMB/RMB).
• Mouse Look: Enables 360° first/third-person FPS camera rotation.

Step 3

Gamepad & Starter Presets

Select MOBA (Honor of Kings/Wild Rift) or FPS (Identity V/Shooters) starter layouts. Plug in Xbox, PlayStation DualSense, or Bluetooth gamepads to map sticks, D-pad, and triggers.

Step 4

Save & Share Profiles

Click Save controls to apply immediately. Use Export draft to generate portable .json keymap files to share with teammates, or Import profiles from the community.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) Macs run iOS games natively? ▼
Yes! PigPort is built specifically for Apple Silicon. Because M-series chips share the ARM architecture with iPhone/iPad, iOS games run natively at full speed without emulator overhead or battery drain.
Where can I find .IPA or .APK files? ▼
For iOS games, use decrypted repositories such as decrypt.day or extract from your personal Apple ID account. For Android, safe repositories like APKPure or APKMirror provide direct .apk/.xapk packages. Check our "Get Games" section above for direct links!
Is playing games via PigPort bannable? ▼
PigPort runs games in clean containers and does not modify core game mechanics. However, some games with strict kernel-level anti-cheat might flag non-standard hardware. We recommend using secondary accounts for heavily monitored competitive games.
Is PigPort completely free? ▼
Yes! PigPort is 100% free and open-source under the MIT license. If you enjoy the project, feel free to support development with a coffee via PayPal (paypal.me/pigtiny) or star the repository on GitHub!
